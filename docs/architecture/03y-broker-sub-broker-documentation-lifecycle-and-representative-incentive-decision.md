### 4.5 The Sub-Broker commission-recipiency ambiguity, IDENTIFIED not guessed

> ⟦ARCHITECT ANALYSIS⟧ **`PF-203` — ⟦PRODUCT-OWNER DECISION⟧ `PO-Y1.H` instructs: *"If the existing

**The ambiguity, stated precisely, in three parts.**

| # | The ambiguity |
|---|---|
| **1** | ⚠ **`PO-Y1.H`'s OWN TEXT ADMITS TWO READINGS AND SELECTS NEITHER.** ***"A Sub-Broker is NOT treated as a normal commission-splitting participant in the same way as Brokers."* **Reading 1: a Sub-Broker may NOT receive builder-side commission at all — the clause is a prohibition softened by *"normal"* and *"in the same way."* **Reading 2: a Sub-Broker MAY receive builder-side commission, but not through the ordinary multi-Broker splitting mechanism — the clause is about the MECHANISM, not about the entitlement.** **BOTH ARE AVAILABLE ON THE WORDS AND NEITHER IS SELECTED HERE** |
| **2** | ⚠ **THE NEXT SENTENCE USES A DIFFERENT WORD, AND THE DIFFERENCE MAY OR MAY NOT BE DELIBERATE.** ***"A Sub-Broker can still receive builder-side incentives."* **Not *commission* — *incentives*.
